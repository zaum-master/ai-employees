# Shared role intake

The buyer has selected a role and supplied a JD or plain-language description.
Use the selected role's `ROLE.md` to identify the work and authority boundary.

Reflect a concise role charter, then ask only:

> What is the first piece of work you want this employee to handle, and where does that work happen today?

Ask a further question only when a role-specific essential fact in `ROLE.md`
is missing. Do not ask for system credentials or a contact list until the buyer
has chosen a work surface and the first useful result is clear.

Render the operating brief with the role purpose, first work item, systems or
evidence it may use, authorized work surfaces, human approval boundary, and
first useful result. Unknown facts remain explicitly unknown.
